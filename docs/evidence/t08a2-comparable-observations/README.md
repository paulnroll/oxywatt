## Review outcomes

A/B should remain separate descriptive candidates. Compare native coverage and a locked matched-support/time/context intersection; do not call differing retained HR distributions a controlled comparison. A-only observations can be useful descriptive records without becoming comparable evidence for B or fitness. Narrow qualification may remove the very fatigued visits of interest, so report excluded visits and context, not just numeric results.

A useful next empirical result would quantify independently acquired within-person visit differences and actual support/abstention under documented equipment/context continuity. It could reject feasibility without CPET. Change validity needs later independently repeated references matched to the intended claim. Smoothing and more synthetic recipes supply neither.
