## Observable and claim boundary

| Quantity | Definition / units | Meaning of an increase |
|---|---|---|
| Observed power at comparable HR | Time-weighted measured external power in a frozen HR band, or interpolated power at a fixed HR anchor; W | More observed external work rate at comparable recorded HR under the declared selection/context, not proof of fitness improvement |
| Power–HR relation | Qualified segment pairs (mean HR bpm, mean power W), their support, dispersion, time-in-ride and context | A change in the observed relation; lag, drift, selection and sensor changes can also move it |
| Experimental index | Optional `100 × session W / baseline W`, dimensionless, baseline explicitly frozen | Relative change of that observation, without physiological calibration |
| Physiological VO₂max | Independently verified maximal cycling oxygen uptake, L/min or mL/kg/min | A distinct physiological claim requiring repeated independent CPET references; neither power–HR output supplies it |

Use report label **Experimental power at comparable HR**, never FTP, VO₂max, aerobic efficiency, or a confidence percentage. Higher is descriptive, not automatically “better”. Physiological fatigue can suppress HR; improvement and impairment need not map monotonically to a single power–HR statistic. A constant offset in a proxy does not establish constant longitudinal bias.

## Minimal candidates and recommendation for review

Both candidates use only qualified, nonoverlapping segments j with paired covered duration d_j (s), time-weighted mean power p_j (W) and HR h_j (bpm). Means are integrals over the **same jointly valid intervals**, not sample-count averages or independent channel means. No HRmax, mass, FTP, oxygen-cost or efficiency conversion is used.

**A — fixed absolute HR band.** Freeze anchor H* (bpm) and half-width b (bpm) before a comparison series. Retain qualified segments with `abs(h_j − H*) ≤ b`. Session result `x_s = Σ(d_j p_j) / Σd_j`, with observed mean HR, HR range, segment count and paired duration reported. Duration weighting describes the retained observation, not confidence. Selection must also satisfy within-segment HR/power stability and drift policy; a mean in-band does not make a ramp stable. Wide bands and changed HR distribution can induce apparent changes: report them and test band sensitivity. No band retuning to maximize a desired trend.

**B — local interpolation at the same anchor.** From the same qualified segment pairs, fit `p_j = a_s + c_s(h_j − H*) + error_j` by duration-weighted least squares, minimizing `Σd_j[p_j − a_s − c_s(h_j − H*)]^2`. Result `x_s = a_s` W, slope c_s W/bpm. Require HR support on both sides of H*, nonzero weighted HR variance, approved conditioning/support/residual checks and positive interpretable slope; otherwise abstain. No extrapolation to H*, HRmax or oxygen uptake. HR measurement error affects the predictor too; ordinary least squares is an explicit experimental assumption, not an unbiased physiological model. No inferential confidence interval from correlated segment residuals.

Recommend **A as the initial descriptive reference, B as a diagnostic comparator**: A needs fewer fit assumptions but can be distribution-sensitive; B corrects local HR location under a linearity assumption but requires broader reliable support. Approval of T08a1 would authorize comparison of both on synthetic inputs, not automatic method selection. A post-experiment owner review may accept neither. Neither formula is copied from a validated passive-ride estimator: these are OxyWatt descriptive proposals, motivated and limited by the primary evidence below.

Reject `mean power / mean HR` (W/bpm) as the primary indicator: it assumes an origin/intercept behavior not established by the evidence and changes with workload distribution. HRmax-normalized anchors and W/kg introduce extra profile dependencies and are deferred, not default alternatives.

## Input/time and comparability policy

Read canonical `ReceiptStamp`, `MetricReading`, `RiderProfileSnapshot`, Collector effects, `CanonicalPowerReplay`, T05a `ExperimentProfile`, and `AnalysisRepository` before implementation. SourceRef identifies an adapter, **not a sensor**. Canonical profiles contain optional mass/configured HRmax/configured FTP; T05a context/calibration evidence is development metadata. Neither sensor identity nor temperature, hydration, fatigue, medication or indoor/outdoor is currently guaranteed by live canonical observations.

- Build paired coverage from committed events/effects at frozen revision R and terminal session snapshot, preserving ingest order, epochs, source/file-time meaning and normalization. Live receipt time is not measurement time. Align on the declared acquisition-time policy; do not infer radio latency from HR response. File-record time stays distinguishable. Do not change power-curve hold-v1 or reuse its TTL as a physiological HR assumption.
- Define a candidate-specific HR hold/expiry and joint-coverage policy explicitly. An event confirms only supported time up to the next reading, break, expiry and committed horizon, whichever is first. No end-of-file extrapolation, interpolation across missing intervals, zero fill or complete-case sample-rate weighting. Zero power is observed but may be nonqualifying work; missing power/HR is absent. Cadence is optional unless a reviewed policy explicitly requires it.
- Warm-up, workload transitions, pauses, loss, restart, profile changes and stale channels break qualification. Restart qualification after each break, including a new settling period. HR lag handling is exclusion of transitions/settling by explicit policy, not guessed shifting of HR to make correlation strongest. Retain elapsed time since known start and since latest transition. Unknown warm-up history cannot be called settled. Synthetic transition boundaries are known by construction; real detection policy is still unvalidated.
- Within candidate windows retain HR and power dispersion and time slopes (bpm/s, W/s), first/last portions and time-in-ride. Exclude unsupported stability/drift regimes under the frozen policy. Short apparently stable HR can still reflect delayed response. Do not “correct” drift into presumed fitness or treat all late-ride windows as equivalent to early ones.
- One scalar per candidate/session/cohort. Sessions need observed start/local end and no interrupted/cold-attached/ambiguous recovery history; linked fragments contribute zero. Unknown identity is not repaired by a new UUID. Segments within one session are dependent; they never increase the independent session/person count.
- Compare the same HR anchor, policy, supported HR range, time-in-ride stratum, source/time semantics, environment and documented sensor/calibration context. Indoor and outdoor are separate; unknown is not assumed equal to either. Synthetic contexts may be explicitly declared in existing fixture/profile evidence. Real comparative admission with unknown sensor/calibration/environment remains blocked; keep observations with context_unverified rather than asserting comparability.
- A known sensor replacement/calibration change creates a cohort boundary; no learned offset correction. Power scale/offset and HR bias can mimic trend. No sensor serial collection is needed; later live context capture requires a separate contract decision. Simulator labels are invented context, not measured evidence.
- Any profile revision in a session splits qualification; conservative comparison cohorts pin the full profile digest. Mass/HRmax changes do not alter A/B algebra but start a new comparison cohort with an explicit configuration-change reason. Demonstrate algebraic invariance separately from cohort exclusion; do not manufacture a mass/HRmax effect on output. No automatic baseline carry-over. - Temperature, fatigue, hydration, illness, medication and caffeine remain unmeasured unless explicit lawful evidence is supplied. Show unknown/confounded status; never infer “unchanged” from missing context or synthesize a correction. Even declared matching context cannot prove physiological comparability.

For executable synthetic selection, use a deterministic nonoverlapping half-open grid of recipe-specified window length measured from the latest known start/resume/qualification reset. Discard incomplete windows; a break resets the grid and settling clock. Compute time-weighted means/variances and least-squares time slopes from joint intervals, recording units and boundary comparisons in the configuration. Do not search arbitrary overlapping windows for the highest power. Recipe configuration must supply every required cutoff, settling duration, TTL, anchor, support rule, numeric tolerance and work limit; absent/unknown policies refuse before calculation. Synthetic scenario knowledge may define transition resets explicitly, but cannot be advertised as a validated real-ride detector.

Refusal reasons distinguish no paired coverage, unsupported timing/policy, unknown warm-up, unsettled response, excessive drift/instability, insufficient anchor support/conditioning, context unverified/incompatible, ambiguous session, changed configuration, resource limit and deleted inputs. ## Session result, aggregation, baseline and smoothing

Keep raw eligible segment pairs and exclusions in the development report, reproducible from retained frozen inputs. Session x_s is already an aggregation of segments; do not call that a trend. Preserve one current raw value or refusal per candidate/session and its eligible duration/HR support, not just a chart point.

For the synthetic experiment, a recipe explicitly names baseline session IDs before outcome calculation. `B = arithmetic mean(x_s for those eligible sessions)` with equal session weights. All named baseline contributors must remain eligible and compatible; otherwise baseline unavailable, no silent replacement. Require positive B before percent indexing. Report `Δ_s = x_s − B` W, and optionally `100(x_s/B − 1)` percent. Baseline contributors, cohort, configuration and as-of time are explicit. These are experiment conventions, not a validated minimum session count or observation horizon. Recipe data sufficiency is not population sample-size evidence.

Primary experiment report is **unsmoothed**. Baseline is a fixed reference set, not a moving average; no rolling aggregate or smoother is needed in this smallest package. Smoothing, stale-time thresholds, rolling baseline and directional alerts are deferred to a separately reviewed T08a policy decision. The older Canvas trend-v0 42/14/3 proposal is not inherited here. Any later smoother must retain raw results, rejected/missing gaps and contributors, and evaluate false direction and delay against the unsmoothed comparator. Silence never implies maintenance, improvement or decline. No confidence interval from smoothed variability.
