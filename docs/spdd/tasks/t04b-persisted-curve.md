## Confirmed coverage policy — proposed canonical-power-hold-v1

Engineering policy, not measured sensor validity: use the persisted session LifecyclePolicy.channelSilenceNanos as the hold ceiling (existing preview: 2 seconds). Require a supported lifecycle-policy version and positive timeout; include the exact version and nanoseconds in parameter provenance. No independently chosen longer power TTL and no resampling/interpolation. Receipt elapsed nanoseconds govern duration; UTC and FILE_RECORD times remain audit provenance only.

A present finite nonnegative power reading in an assigned recording segment opens a held value at t. Confirm [t, min(next power event, persisted power break, expiry, known terminal boundary, committed clock horizon)) only as later committed effects advance acquisition time. The durable horizon is the greatest same-epoch receipt elapsed time or coverage-break detectedAtNanos in the committed stream so far. A checkpoint lastReceipt does not advance on a timer-only update; a no-op advanceTime stores no additional usable time. Do not invent that missing clock evidence. No interval is emitted for zero duration. The last reading alone confirms no future time. A next equal-valued reading refreshes expiry without changing watts; zero is valid coverage. Missing/invalid power closes at its timestamp and breaks continuity. HR/cadence/profile absence does not break power; their committed updates may advance the horizon but cannot refresh power expiry. Never advance beyond the persisted clock horizon just because the software knows a timeout value.

Process each revision/index in order. Within an update: advance held coverage to due breaks/deadlines, apply breaks and terminal changes, then its envelope/new reading. Due timers precede observations at equal timestamp, matching V18/T03a; expiry at exactly t breaks coverage before a present reading at t, even with no positive gap. Equal-time observations retain ingest order; intermediate readings contribute zero time. A pause then resume then reading at t cannot reconnect pre-pause coverage. Flush timers are not new observations or power refreshes. Explicit breaks call T04a.breakCoverage even at adjacent boundaries. If an expiry would be required by a committed horizon but is absent/inconsistent in stored lifecycle effects, fail with an unsupported/inconsistent-input reason rather than silently contradict T03a.

Pause closes coverage at the pause boundary; paused metrics remain persisted but cannot open an interval. Resume needs a new present power reading in its new segment. Silent power gap ends at last power receipt + persisted timeout; later reading starts independent coverage. Local Idle closes at its committed boundary and means ENDED_LOCALLY, not native saved. Stop/EOF/error close only to the committed terminal horizon, mark interrupted and never manufacture Idle. Discard any uncommitted tail. On process death without a terminal clock advance, cap old-epoch coverage at its last durable horizon; a new-epoch recovery timestamp cannot extend the old value. Recovery effects update partial status but cannot supply old-epoch elapsed duration.

Never bridge session UUIDs, epochs, segments, missing/expiry, loss or interruption. Keep winners already observed in the selected session; never merge linked ambiguous fragments. Loss metadata breaks coverage no later than its first lost receipt in the same epoch, before subsequent admitted inputs; no hold may cross that loss. Terminal status/origin/ambiguity are stored beside records, not inferred from numeric availability. Interrupted, cold-attached and recovered sessions may display partial observations; none enters aggregates. Even locally ended synthetic sessions remain demo-only.

## Work bounds and diagnostic readout

T04a's direct integration can be quadratic for a long incoming interval after dense short intervals; bounded memory alone is insufficient. T04b1 is a terminal batch diagnostic on the existing worker, never a per-callback or UI-thread operation. Keep T04a unchanged unless a separately reviewed correctness fix is necessary. Coalesce only exactly adjacent equal watts within uninterrupted identity.

Proposed `power-replay-budget-v1`: engine cap 16384, paged replay 256, one held value, nine winners, and 20,000,000 cumulative conservative fragment-work units per session. Before each engine.accept, reserve 9*(n+3)*(n+1) units using checked arithmetic, where n is retained intervals from its last snapshot. This upper-bounds candidate count times scanned fragments for that call, including a long interval; charge even if actual work is lower. If reservation exceeds remaining budget, do not call accept: stop the scan, preserve prior winners, store resource-limited/not-fully-searched status and no completeness claim. Include this wrapper limit separately from engine saturation. Test the bound against actual evaluated-window/integrated-fragment counters, adversarial dense-then-long input and repeated saturation. It intentionally rejects some large safe inputs; higher-rate/long mixed rides need later optimization, not silently higher limits.

Page/hash passes are streaming with cancellation checks between pages and intervals. Record input/effect/interval counts, reserved and actual engine work, retained plateau and host elapsed time for short demo, 3600-second constant trace, four-hour 1 Hz alternating trace and dense-then-long stress. Resource-limited outcomes are acceptable only where declared in the fixture expectations. No host timing is a Karoo CPU/RSS/battery claim.

Existing preview adds a resource-string-based curve section: DEMO/FIT REPLAY origin, session ID, locally ended/partial/ambiguous state, generation/status, input revision/hash, nine duration values or reasons and a prominent incomplete-search label. Keep existing collection diagnostics. Reopen/recreate reads durable newest results, with no automatic resumption or repeated requests on Activity creation. Calculation triggers once after terminal commit; explicit demo rerun creates a separate session. Storage failure shows unsaved/error state. No new history screen, speed/fault panel or live startup. Preserve `.preview`, separate synthetic databases and disabled live extension; release excludes controls/fixtures. A longer synthetic fixture includes an observed Idle/start, six seconds of refreshed 200 W and local end, giving exactly 200 W at 5 s, longer durations unavailable.
